# Ads

## Purpose
Shows a rewarded or an interstitial ad on request, from any module, and answers whoever asked -
rewarded, dismissed, not ready, failed, skipped - without the game knowing which ad SDK is doing
the showing. The mediation SDK is a plug on `AdsServiceRoot`; the module owns the policy the SDK
leaves to the app.

## Using it
1. `AdsServiceRoot` into the boot scene, Initialize Order -84. `CD_Ads` ships filed on it:
   `InitializeOnLaunch`, `InterstitialMinIntervalSeconds`, `ShowTimeoutSeconds`.
2. One plug on the Root's Sub Context Types, AutoSetup on: `AppLovinMaxAdsServiceContext`
   (with its own asset filed in Shared Scriptables - see its card), or your own
   `[AllowAsSubContext]` context that plugs your own `IAdsProvider`.
3. Bind the shows as steps of the module that decided them:
   `.ToSequence<IAdsService.Commands.ShowRewarded>("chest").ToSequence<GrantChestCommand>()` -
   the grant runs only when the reward was earned; and
   `.ToSequence<IAdsService.Commands.ShowInterstitial>("level_end").ToSequence<OpenLevelEndScreenCommand>()` -
   the flow goes on whatever happened. A Command that needs both paths injects `IAdsService` and
   reads the `AdResultVO` in its callback.
4. A watch-ad button reads `IsReady(AdFormat.Rewarded)` and follows `Outgoing.ReadyChanged`.
5. A Connector carries `Outgoing.Opened` / `Closed` to the audio module and `Outgoing.RevenuePaid`
   to the module that logs analytics.
6. `SetAdsRemoved(true)` after the purchase and at boot from the save; `SetConsent` from the
   consent flow; `SetMuted` beside the sound toggle.

## Decisions
- **A socket, one plug.** A mediation SDK is one; a second `Plug` is refused with an error.
- **The module owns the policy, the plug owns the SDK calls**: one ad of each format kept loaded,
  a failed load retried at 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64 seconds and then every 64, the next ad loaded after
  every close, interstitials paced and skipped once ads are removed, a show nobody answers in
  `ShowTimeoutSeconds` failed.
- **The answer goes to the asker.** The rewarded step releases only on `Rewarded` and stops
  otherwise; the interstitial step releases on every outcome with the result handed on.
- **Outgoing only.** `ReadyChanged`, `Opened`, `Closed`, `Failed`, `RevenuePaid`; there is no
  Incoming, because the way into a Service is its interface.
- **Consent is handed on, never resolved.** Three flags, applied before the SDK initializes when
  known, at once when it changes.
- **Nothing persisted.** Ads removed, consent and muted are re-applied by the game at boot.

## Known gaps
- No banner; `AdFormat` leaves `2` for it.
- No ATT prompt and no UMP form here; MAX's own consent flow or a consent module asks.
- No `SetUserId` for reward validation; no typed ad-revenue event.
- One plug shipped; LevelPlay, AdMob and Unity Ads fit behind `IAdsProvider` in about 120 lines.
- A `Displayed` with no `Closed` holds the show for ever; nothing shipped does that.
- No panel: the Device Debugger's Ads rows are the buttons.
