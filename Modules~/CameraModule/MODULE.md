# CameraModule

## Purpose
Registers the scene's cameras, switches between them, and moves the live one to a target or a remembered position.

## Concepts
camera, switch camera, move camera, zoom, camera distance, camera target, follow, remember position, CameraName, CameraCVO, ICameraService

## Decisions
- **One rig, one view.** Every camera of a scene sits under the Root's `CameraManager`, and its
  `CameraAdapterView` lists them all - a `CameraName` to its `CinemachineCamera` - so they register
  together when the scene loads and leave together when it unloads. A camera carries no view of its
  own. HitNPoP's rig held its six cameras this way; the single-camera view shipped beside it was
  never used and is gone.
- **Each camera follows a target of its own** under `CameraManager`, and a camera is moved by
  moving that target: `MoveCamera` glides it and the camera follows, the way HitNPoP's
  `MoveCurrentCameraTarget` did with DOTween - here without a tween library. `SetDistance` zooms
  the same way, easing the live camera's position composer distance, as HitNPoP's
  `SetCameraDistance` did.
- **A Service with no public signals.** `ICameraService` is the only way in: a game injects it,
  or binds `ICameraService.Commands.Switch`, `MoveCamera`, `SetDistance` and `RememberPosition` as
  steps. No Connector is needed.
- **`CameraName` is the game's.** The card's `Extend:` line hands the enum to the game: it adds its
  cameras with the next free number, and an update asks before touching an edited copy.
